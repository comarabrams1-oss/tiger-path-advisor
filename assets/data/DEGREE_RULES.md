# Global & Intercultural Learning mapping

Source: Benedict College Master Catalogue 2025-2026 (010826), general education
chart on pages 102-103, foreign-language descriptions on page 183, and Computer
Science program of study on page 286. This mapping was confirmed by the student
for the existing demo academic profile; the profile's catalog-year label is
preserved rather than silently migrating all degree requirements to another year.

The category contains exactly nine credits:

- African American History: HIST 130, three credits, mandatory.
- Intermediate foreign language: AR 233, FS 233 or SP 233, three credits, mandatory.
- Approved cultural/social-science elective: one approved three-credit course.

The data model represents these as three components, each audited separately.
It does not add a separate nine-credit requirement on top of language, and does
not allow additional electives to replace mandatory history or language. HIST
130 is not reused as the elective. The explicit elective options follow pages
102-103; known other 100/200-level HIST courses are enumerated from the catalogue.
A broad subject such as philosophy or art is not automatic approval for every
course within that subject.

In the current sample record HIST 130 and MUS 130 are completed and AR 233 is
in progress. Thus six credits are completed and three are in progress. The
semester planner does not reserve another Global course. The 120-credit degree
minimum and the student's earned/in-progress credit totals are unchanged.
