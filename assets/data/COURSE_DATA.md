# Course section snapshots

Spring 2027 was captured from Tiger Portal on October 25, 2026 using the
2026-2027 - Spring term, Undergraduate division, all departments, Open or Full
status, and no course, faculty, time, day, campus, or building restriction.
All 25 result groups were captured: 24 groups of 20 sections and a final group
of 4. The resulting 484 unique sections cover 362 courses across 55 subjects.
442 sections run January 11–May 7, 18 run January 11–March 5, and 24 run
March 15–May 7, 2027. 478 have open seats; 89 have no primary meeting time.

Only the selected term and course-table text were saved to the private capture.
The published JSON is the parser's section-field output; it includes no portal
links, authentication fields, account details, or registration actions.

This is an undergraduate snapshot, not a live registration feed. It includes
full-semester and block courses. Graduate offerings were not captured.
Unscheduled or TBA meetings require confirmation with the college. Counts do
not imply that every course in the degree catalog is offered this term.

The Fall asset was regenerated from the existing 27-page sanitized capture,
with compound section identifiers corrected. It contains 529 sections across
356 courses. Its seat availability reflects the older capture.
