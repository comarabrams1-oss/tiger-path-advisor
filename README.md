# Tiger Path Advisor

Course planning and degree progress for Benedict College students. The app uses a demo sign-in and imported course offerings.

## Team 1 student progress demo

1. Sign in as a demo student and choose **View transcript** from Home. Use **Enter final grades** to update a semester, or **Add course** to record a new attempt or accepted transfer credit. Transcript edits update credits, class standing, GPA, degree requirements, and prerequisites. Recorded attempts follow the [Benedict College Catalogue](https://benedict.edu/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/2025-2026-Benedict-College-Master-Catalogue-010826.pdf#page=88) grading and repeat rules. Demo records may include cumulative credit balances beyond their visible course history.
2. Open **Graduation pathway** to see semester recommendations, a completion estimate when the mapped requirements can be planned, and unresolved prerequisites. Expand **Compare a graduation target** to check a date against the path and credit limit.
3. Use **Readiness** to record career and graduate-school milestones, evidence, due dates, and a graduate-program GPA goal. Open **Review with advisor** from Home to see the combined report and copy a summary.

The demo uses local browser or device storage and four degree mappings. Entries in the planning record are student-entered. The Fall 2026 and Spring 2027 sections are snapshots. Later terms use tentative availability, and the roadmap flags elective approvals and catalogue gaps for review. Ask Tiger Path receives computed degree and readiness statuses for relevant questions.

## Course information and interview practice

Ask Tiger Path uses plain-language course summaries from the [Benedict College Catalogue 2025–2026](https://benedict.edu/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/2025-2026-Benedict-College-Master-Catalogue-010826.pdf). Course content is independent of the imported section snapshots and the app's degree rules.

Readiness uses the six questions from [Kamya's Interview Prep app](https://studio.code.org/projects/applab/3acef4f8-55d8-49b3-8dfc-2cda26f8b24e). The same Gemini server endpoint rates practice answers. The latest feedback for each question and the milestone checklist are saved locally by demo profile.
